Overview

This project demonstrates a hybrid workflow spanning 3D modeling, VFX compositing, and game development. Centered around a "Wheeled Cactus," the character served a dual purpose: it was first animated and composited into live-action footage using motion tracking in Blender, and subsequently integrated into Unreal Engine 5 as a fully playable game avatar.

Development Pipeline

  • 3D Modeling & Sculpting: The base mesh was constructed in Blender using primitives, followed by detailed sculpting of thorns and organic surface details to enhance realism.
  • Texturing & Animation: After realistic shading and texturing, a custom animation rig was implemented to simulate the character's unique wheeled movement mechanics.
  • VFX Integration: Utilizing camera motion tracking, the animated 3D character was seamlessly composited into real-world footage, ensuring accurate lighting and shadow interaction.
  • Unreal Engine Implementation: The asset was imported into UE5 as a playable first-person character, serving as the core entity for the game interaction.
  • Blueprint Logic: Game mechanics, including point-collection systems and movement state controls, were fully implemented using Unreal's Blueprint visual scripting system.
