Overview

This project is a 3D study based on Vermeer's The Girl with the Pearl Earring. It was created as an early exercise in Blender to practice blocking, proportion, and the translation of a familiar two-dimensional image into three-dimensional form.

My Contribution

I rebuilt the model from scratch in Blender while following a guided tutorial as a learning framework. The focus was not on original concept design, but on understanding Blender's interface, viewport navigation, shape construction, and basic modeling workflow.

Reference tutorial by Kur Tips

Process and Outcome

  • Blocking Practice: The main objective was to simplify an organic subject into basic geometric forms such as cubes, spheres, and cylinders.
  • Blender Fundamentals: Through the project, I learned core navigation, transformation tools, and the relationship between reference material and 3D form.
  • Early 3D Workflow: As one of my first 3D exercises, the project marked an important starting point in developing my understanding of digital modeling.
