Overview

Lemon Season is an independent podcast collaboratively produced between Germany and China. Together with my co-host, I contribute to topic development, audio production, visual design, and publishing. The show explores friendship, identity, and early adulthood from a Gen Z perspective.

My Role

  • Topic Development: proposing and shaping episode ideas together with the co-host
  • Recording: recording my own audio track and preparing episode materials
  • Audio Editing: performing rough cuts and episode preparation using Xiaoyuzhou's web-based editing tools
  • Visual Design: creating episode cover artwork in Procreate
  • Publishing & Documentation: writing show notes, updating episode information, and publishing new releases

Selected Topics

  • How do we waste our twenties?
  • When spring comes, emotions unlock
  • When We Say Goodbye with All Our Might - Is Separation Anxiety Our Fate?
  • Growing up between expectation and reality
