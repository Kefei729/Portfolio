About Me

Hello! I'm Kefei. I am a Digital Media student at the University of Bremen with a passion for the intersection of code and design.

Rather than limiting myself to a single discipline, I enjoy exploring diverse mediums, ranging from 3D modeling and XR development to interactive web design and audio storytelling. My goal is to combine technical logic with artistic expression to bring creative ideas to life.

Podcast: Lemon Season

Together with a friend, I produce "Lemon Season," a Chinese podcast where we talk openly and honestly about the challenges and joys of growing up. We share personal stories, give tips, and create a space for authentic conversations.
