About Me

I'm Kefei, a Digital Media student at the University of Bremen with a focus on interactive technologies, real-time 3D, and digital storytelling.

My projects range from XR experiences and Unreal Engine development to custom web applications and independent media production. I enjoy combining technical problem-solving with narrative design to create engaging digital experiences.

Geh mit Hoelderlin: VR Poetry Experience

Unreal Engine 5 Meta Quest 3 Blueprint Interfaces Interactive Poetry

Developed an interactive VR poetry experience in Unreal Engine 5 for Meta Quest 3. The project translates selected lines by Friedrich Hoelderlin into a stylized forest landscape through spatial interaction, sound, and visual atmosphere.
